## Why I Started This Blog

As an engineer, I spend most of my time solving problems through code. But I've found that **writing** is just as important as coding — it forces you to organize your thoughts, reflect on what you've learned, and share knowledge with others.

This blog is built with [Astro](https://astro.build), a modern static site generator that makes it easy to write content in Markdown while keeping the site fast and lightweight.

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

- **System Design** —My insights from system design patterns
- **Algorithm & Leetcode Solution** — My LeetCode problem solutions and algorithm organization
- **Tools & Productivity** — tools and workflows that make me more effective
- **More to go**
